PUKAAR High Alert receives emergency SOS from your loved ones.

When they trigger PUKAAR, this app:
• Wakes the phone and rings like an alarm clock
• Shows over the lock screen
• Displays live location, battery, and help numbers
• Keeps re-ringing until you respond

Built for reliability on real phones (including Oppo and Xiaomi):
• Instant FCM push wake
• Background guard + polling if push is delayed
• Works even if you have not opened the app for a long time

Sign in once with the phone number saved as a trusted contact in PUKAAR. Then leave Alert Guard on and set battery to Unrestricted.

PUKAAR High Alert is the trusted-contact companion to the PUKAAR personal safety app.
