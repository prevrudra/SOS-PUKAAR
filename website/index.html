Built for real emergencies

PUKAAR helps you trigger SOS, notify trusted contacts over WhatsApp and High Alert, keep live location flowing, and find nearby emergency services. It does not guarantee rescue — it helps people reach you faster.

  1. 01

    SOS when every second counts

    Silent emergency path for danger, plus a calmer Help path when you need assistance without full alarm.

  2. 02

    Trusted contacts, verified

    Verified contacts get your alert with location, battery, and network status so they can act quickly.

  3. 03

    High Alert on their phone

    Contacts can run High Alert for a full-screen siren when you need them — even if the phone is locked.

  4. 04

    Nearby help from GPS

    Nearest police, hospital, and ambulance details based on your live location during an emergency.
