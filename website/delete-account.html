Delete your PUKAAR account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Operated by AXISPOINT INNOVATIONS PRIVATE LIMITED (GSTIN 09ABECA8628G1ZG).
Deletion requests are handled under our Privacy Policy.

What gets deleted

  • Your PUKAAR account profile and phone login
  • Trusted contacts you added
  • Emergency history and related evidence we hold for your account (subject to legal holds)
  • High Alert device registrations tied to your number, where applicable

What may be retained briefly

We may retain limited records needed for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance, then delete or anonymise them. WhatsApp / SMS messages already delivered to contacts cannot be recalled from their devices.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@pukaaralert.com from your registered number, or
  2. Submit the form below with the phone number used in PUKAAR.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 7–14 business days.

If your mail app does not open, write to contact@pukaaralert.com with subject PUKAAR Account Deletion Request and your registered number.

In-app path

Where available in the app: Menu → Settings / Account → Delete account (or contact support). This web page remains the official Play Console / public deletion URL.
